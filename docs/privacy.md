# Compass Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

Compass is a school community app maintained by Lucas Chen. It helps members of a school connect, share campus updates and manage their school activities.

## What Compass uses

- **Account and school information.** Firebase Authentication handles your sign-in credentials, email verification and account ID. Compass stores your name, email address, grade and school membership. When you use Google sign-in where offered, Google provides your verified email and may provide your display name.
- **Content and connections.** Compass stores the posts, photos, comments and messages you choose to share. It also stores friend requests, accepted friendships, blocks, reports, saved posts, interests and guide and graduation progress used to run the app.
- **School registration.** When you apply to add a school, Compass stores your name, role, email, the representative's contact email and the school details for review.
- **Calendars and local settings.** Imported calendars, subscription links, appearance settings, and local-only profile photos and drafts stay in your browser or on your device. When you refresh a calendar subscription, Compass sends its URL to the provider. The web version sends it through the Compass calendar gateway to retrieve the feed. Compass does not store your private calendar link in Firebase.

## Who can see your information

Members of your Compass school can see content posted to that school. If you enable the directory, they can search your display name. If you choose to post anonymously, Compass hides your display name in the feed and keeps the account ID for moderation and ownership.

Direct conversations are available to the two accepted friends in the conversation. Firebase stores the messages. The conversations do not use end-to-end encryption. Authorized Compass operators review new shared photo albums before publication and can review safety reports and related information to resolve abuse and support requests.

Google Firebase provides authentication and cloud storage. The hosted web version uses the hosting service and calendar gateway to serve the app. Compass does not use your school information or content for advertising, sell your personal information or track your activity across other companies' apps and sites.

## Your choices and deletion

Use **Profile → Settings → Delete account** to permanently delete your Compass account and associated cloud data, including shared posts, comments, photos and direct conversations. School applicants can request deletion from their signed-in application screen. An account deletion request can take up to seven days. Check the welcome screen on the device that requested deletion for confirmation. That device also clears the account data stored by the app there. You can remove local data on other devices through their app or browser settings.

Compass keeps account data while your account is active. After account deletion, a temporary write lock expires after 24 hours and an anonymous status receipt expires after 30 days. Those records support completion and confirmation.

For privacy requests or questions, contact [noreply@lucaschen.photography](mailto:noreply@lucaschen.photography). For reporting and other help, see [Compass Support](support.md).
