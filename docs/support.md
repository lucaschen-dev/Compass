# Compass Support

For account access, privacy or safety issues, email [noreply@lucaschen.photography](mailto:noreply@lucaschen.photography). Include the school name, the type of device and a description of what happened. Keep passwords, private calendar links, student records and private messages out of support email.

## Report or block someone

New shared photo albums go to a moderator before they appear in the school feed. Use the menu next to a published post, campus photo, comment or direct message to report it. Compass sends the report privately for review. Block someone to remove their content from your view and stop new direct messages or friend requests. Manage blocks in privacy settings.

## Delete your account

Open **Profile → Settings → Delete account** and follow the confirmation steps. If you have a school registration account, open your signed-in application and select **Delete account**. Check the welcome screen on the same device for the result.

## Community rules

Treat other members with respect. Compass prohibits bullying, harassment, hate speech, threats, sexually explicit or exploitative material, sharing personal information without permission and posting photos of others without permission. Compass may remove content or restrict accounts that violate these rules. Use the in-app report control so the moderation team can review the relevant item.

See the [Compass Privacy Policy](privacy.md) for information about data use and deletion.
